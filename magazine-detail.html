Festive Travel & Emerging European Gateways

In this edition of India Outbound, we delve deep into the unprecedented surge in outbound departures ahead of the Diwali and winter holiday season. Highlights include exclusive interviews with European tourism boards, aviation route expansions from Indian tier-1 and tier-2 metros, and a deep-dive into luxury cruise partnerships in the Caribbean and Mediterranean.
