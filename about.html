India Outbound is India’s only B2B travel media platform, that encompasses a print magazine, a website, an online TV channel and dynamic social media platforms, entirely dedicated to the promotion of foreign destinations and products in India.

India Outbound is a Media India Group brand

MISSION
  • Provide trending news, analysis and reports of the Indian Outbound travel industry.
  • Influence the industry with in-depth information and serve as a tool and platform for the outbound industry decision makers to make their strategies.
  • Offer a multi-support platform for global destinations and products to promote themselves to a targeted audience and increase their market share.
Vision
  • Become the most accurate, professional and serious source of information on outbound travel from India.
The content
  • The magazine and the related digital media assets (Videos, social media and online reports) specialise in bringing original, well-researched and thought-provoking articles to the readers.
  • We have a comprehensive coverage of all the news and analysis of the market trends, strategies and other development through print articles, web articles, social media news and video features.
  • India Outbound also brings to the Indian travel trade the latest technologies, tools, trends and strategies being adopted by destinations as well as travel trade professionals around the world and how they may be adapted for India.
Besides revealing the hidden treasures of global destinations, India Outbound gives indepth analysis on various issues impacting the tourism industry, not just in India but also overseas.
Ranvir Nayar,

Founder and Managing Editor of India Outbound (Media India Group)

We reach out to the Indian B2B industry and travel enthusiasts through :

Bi-Monthly Magazine

Published by Media India Group, India Outbound is a B2B Magazine, published every month and distributed free to the Indian Travel Trade. Anyone can subscribe to the online version by following minor registration steps and/or subscribe to the print version at a minimal cost.

IO Newsletter

An exclusive weekly digest of outbound travel trade news, analysed and selected by our reporters that are sent out to +5000 members of the travel trade industry in India. IO Newsletter also carries special features with focus on region or themes sent out occasionally.

Website

India Outbound website is modern, dynamic and full of information. Based on your interests, as a travel trade professional or simply a travel enthusiastic, we have categorised our sections in a way that you find what all what you seek in terms of global destination coverage with an Indian touch and keeping in mind the travel requirements from India.

Online TV Channel

The only online TV channel dedicated to news and features of global destinations with an Indian angle. With an archive of nearly 300 videos featuring carrying key interviews with decision makers of destinations and tourism companies from all over the world as well as coverage of unique and offbeat destinations, event reports, roadshows and much more.

Social Media

Combining a band of well over 150,000 followers and with a monthly reach of over 5 million and even higher during special promotions. You’ll find the most timely and professional news shared not only on our pages but also on various B2B travel groups on Facebook, Twitter and Instagram.

As the world of travel evolves thanks to changing consumer preferences, especially in the post-pandemic era, India Outbound will continuously strive to bring the latest information about developments from around the world for our travel trade partners as well as the travellers.
Varsha Singh,

Editorial Director & Product Head of India Outbound (Media India Group)
